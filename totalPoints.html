Each sport's KPI rating is rescaled to a 0–100 grade so the totals stay readable. Total sums those grades, so schools fielding more varsity sports naturally rack up more of it; Avg divides that by sports fielded instead, so a school with fewer, stronger programs isn't penalized for breadth; Top 8 counts how many of a school's sports rank in the national top 8, rewarding elite finishes over breadth or consistency. #1s/T4/T8 count how many of a school's sports rank #1, top-4, or top-8 nationally. "D1 (Combined)" merges FBS, FCS, and D1 (No Football) into one list. Click a school to look it up sport-by-sport.
