Each sport's KPI rating is rescaled to a 0–100 grade so the totals stay readable (values are rounded to whole numbers for display only). Total sums those grades, so schools fielding more varsity sports naturally rack up more of it; Avg divides that by sports fielded instead, so a school with fewer, stronger programs isn't penalized for breadth; Top 8 sums only a school's 8 highest-rated sports, capping how much a 9th or 10th sport can add. #1s/T4 separately count how many of a school's sports rank #1 or top-4 nationally. "D1 (Combined)" merges FBS, FCS, and D1 (No Football) into one list. Click a school to look it up sport-by-sport.
