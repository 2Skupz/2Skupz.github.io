Method

  • Expected margin for the home team is PPI difference plus 2.5. Neutral-site games (Munich, Mexico City) and the Super Bowl get no home edge.
  • Ratings are held fixed for the rest of the season. For predictions, each PPI is shrunk toward 0 as PPI × n / (n + 12), where n is games played (12 phantom games of league average). The PPI column shows the raw rating.
  • Ties are ignored in future games. Overtime is not modeled.
  • Seeds 1–4 are division winners and 5–7 are wild cards. The top seed gets a bye, and higher seeds host.
  • Tiebreakers follow NFL order, without net points: head-to-head, division, common games, conference, strength of victory, strength of schedule, then a coin flip. Multi-team ties are simplified.
  • Week 16–17 flex games are on tentative dates and may move, but the matchups are set.
